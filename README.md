## What it is

`main.py` writes one short plan (279 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`: an AI agent turns $100 into the most money in 30 days by selling verified lead lists to US roofing firms — scrape 3,000 firms from Google Maps, verify owner emails, cold-email 150/day with 25 free leads, sell 500-lead packs for $99 in USDC. Expected outcome: ~15 sales ≈ $1,485. No KYC, no meetings, no human steps.

## The plan

> Day 1: spend $30 on Apify+Hunter credits, scrape 3,000 US roofing firms from Google Maps, verify emails. Days 2-30: email 150/day, give 25 free leads, sell 500 verified leads for $99 in USDC. Expect ~15 sales = $1,485. Edge: verified niche data, paid in USDC, no KYC or meetings.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It creates `/output/` if needed and writes `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, deterministic — same bytes on every run). No network access.
